# The bloofer lady

## Overview
Small children on Hampstead Heath go missing at dusk and are found next
morning with tiny wounds in their throats. They say a "bloofer lady"
asked them to walk with her.

## Setup
- Chapter 13: the cutting from *The Westminster Gazette*, which reads it
  as a children's game.

### Chapter 14
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:14 -->
- Van Helsing reads the cutting, and tells Seward that Lucy made the
  wounds.

## Payoff
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:15 -->
The payoff starts in chapter 15, a chapter before the thread is paid
off, when Van Helsing first shows Seward the empty coffin. Over chapters
15 and 16 he shows Seward, then Arthur and Morris, that the bloofer lady
is Lucy Un-Dead.

### The stake
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:16 -->
Arthur frees her. See [[lucy-is-laid-to-rest]].
