# Jonathan's silence

## Overview
What happened to Jonathan after he climbed down the castle wall? The
reader leaves him on the wall at the end of chapter 4 and doesn't hear of
him again for four chapters.

## Setup
- Chapter 4: the Count makes Harker write letters dated weeks ahead, so
  that no one at home will worry when he stops writing. Then the journal
  stops.
- Chapter 6: at Whitby, Mina has had no word from him since a short note
  dated from the castle. "Another week gone, and no news from Jonathan."

## Payoff
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:8 -->
In chapter 8 Sister Agatha writes from a hospital in Buda-Pesth: he has
been there for nearly six weeks with a violent brain fever. Mina goes to
him at once.

### The wedding
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:9 -->
She marries him there. See [[mina-and-jonathan-marry]].
