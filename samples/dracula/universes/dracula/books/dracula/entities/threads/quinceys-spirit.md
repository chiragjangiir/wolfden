# Quincey's spirit

## Overview
The Harkers' son is born on the anniversary of Quincey Morris's death.
"His mother holds, I know, the secret belief that some of our brave
friend's spirit has passed into him."

## Setup
Planted in the closing Note, the last page of the novel, and never paid
off. That makes it the open thread a sequel would pick up.

## Payoff
<!-- wolfden: status=undecided -->
What, if anything, does the date of the boy's birth mean?

### A mother's comfort
<!-- wolfden: status=candidate -->
Nothing supernatural. The coincidence of the date is a comfort to a
family who lost a friend, and the Note says no more than that.

### Something passed on
<!-- wolfden: status=candidate -->
Mina drank the Count's blood, and her son is born on the day the Count
died as well as the day Morris did. Something passed into the child, and
not only from Morris.
