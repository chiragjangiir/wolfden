# Mrs. Westenra

## Overview
[[lucy-westenra|Lucy]]'s widowed mother. Her heart is weak, and she knows
she hasn't long to live, so the others keep bad news from her.

## Arc
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:11 -->
Not knowing what they are for, she throws away the garlic flowers in
Lucy's room and opens the window. On the night a wolf breaks the window
she dies of fright on Lucy's bed. See [[the-wolf-at-hillingham]].
