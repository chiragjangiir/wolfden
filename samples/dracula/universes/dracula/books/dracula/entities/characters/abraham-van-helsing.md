# Abraham Van Helsing

## Overview
A professor from Amsterdam, "M. D., D. Ph., D. Lit., etc., etc.," and
[[john-seward]]'s old teacher. Seward calls him in to see
[[lucy-westenra|Lucy]].

## Voice
Formal, broken English ("friend John," "Madam Mina"), long speeches, and
kindness under it all.

### Laughter
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:13 -->
After Lucy's funeral his grief comes out of him as wild laughter.

## Wound
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:13 -->
He mentions, once, a son who would have been Arthur's age had he lived,
and a wife "dead to me, but alive by Church's law," her wits gone.

## Arc
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:10 -->
He suspects the truth about Lucy early, and fights with garlic and blood
without saying what he fears.

### The leader
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:18 -->
He lays out the rules of the Un-Dead to the band (see
[[rules-of-the-un-dead]]) and leads the hunt.

### The castle
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:27 -->
He keeps Mina safe in a holy circle and destroys [[the-weird-sisters]] in
the castle, alone.
