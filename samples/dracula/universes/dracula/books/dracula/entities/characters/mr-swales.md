# Mr. Swales

## Overview
A very old man of [[whitby]] who sits on the churchyard seat and scoffs at
the gravestones, many of which stand over empty graves of men lost at sea.

## Voice
Thick Yorkshire dialect, which Mina tries to write down as she hears it.

## Arc
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:7 -->
Two nights after the *Demeter* comes in, he is found dead on the seat
with his neck broken and a look of horror on his face.
