# R. M. Renfield

## Overview
A patient at [[seward-asylum]], aged 59, of great strength and moods that
swing from calm to violence. [[john-seward]] makes him his special study.

## Backstory
Nothing is said of his life before the asylum, except that he speaks like
an educated gentleman when he chooses.

## Want
Lives. He eats flies, feeds the flies to spiders, the spiders to
sparrows, and asks for a kitten.

### Zoöphagous
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:6 -->
Seward names him "zoöphagous": a life-eating maniac.

## Relationships
- [[john-seward]]: his doctor and jailer.

### The Master
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:8 -->
- [[count-dracula]]: "I am here to do Your bidding, Master." See
  [[renfields-master]].

## Arc
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:18 -->
He begs to be released before the night the Count comes for Mina, and is
refused.

### Death
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:21 -->
He tries to stop the Count reaching Mina and is broken against the floor.
Dying, he tells the band how he let the Count in.
