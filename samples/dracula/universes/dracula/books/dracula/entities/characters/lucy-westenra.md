# Lucy Westenra

## Overview
[[mina-harker|Mina]]'s dearest friend: lively, affectionate, and much
admired. She tells Mina about her suitors in chapter 5.

## Appearance
<!-- wolfden: until=dracula:15 -->
Fair, sweet-faced, and slight.

### Decline
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:8 -->
From August on she grows pale and thin, with two small red marks on her
throat that never heal.

## Appearance
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:15 until=dracula:16 -->
Days after her funeral she lies in her coffin more beautiful than in
life, with red lips and sharp teeth.

## Appearance
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:16 -->
Seen walking at night, her sweetness has turned "to adamantine,
heartless cruelty." After the stake her face is her own again, as she
was in life.

## Want
To marry [[arthur-holmwood|Arthur]], and to be kind to the two men she
refuses.

## Relationships
- [[mina-harker]]: her closest friend.
- [[mrs-westenra]]: her mother, whose heart is failing.
- [[arthur-holmwood]]: her fiancé.
- [[john-seward]] and [[quincey-morris]]: suitors she refuses on the same
  day, who stay her friends.

## Arc
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:8 -->
She walks in her sleep at Whitby and is found on the churchyard seat with
a dark figure bending over her. Four men give her their blood, and it
isn't enough.

### Death
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:12 -->
She dies at [[hillingham]] on 20 September. Over her body Van Helsing
says: "It is only the beginning!"

### The Un-Dead
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:13 until=dracula:17 -->
Children on Hampstead Heath are lured away by a "bloofer lady" and found
with small wounds in their throats. See [[the-bloofer-lady]].

### Rest
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:16 -->
Arthur drives the stake on 29 September. See [[lucy-is-laid-to-rest]].
