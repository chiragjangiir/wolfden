# Count Dracula

## Overview
A Transylvanian nobleman who buys an English estate through
[[jonathan-harker]]'s firm and receives him at [[castle-dracula]]:
"Welcome to my house! Enter freely and of your own will!"

## Appearance
A tall old man, clean-shaven but for a long white moustache, with a
strong aquiline face, pointed ears, sharp white teeth, hair growing on
his palms, and rank breath. After feeding he looks younger.

## Appearance
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:13 -->
In London he looks far younger, with a black moustache and a pointed
beard. Jonathan knows him at once.

## Backstory
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:3 -->
He boasts to Harker of the Székely wars as if he had fought in all of
them.

### Who he was
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:18 -->
Van Helsing names him as "that Voivode Dracula who won his name against
the Turk," a soldier and statesman who learned his secrets at the
Scholomance.

## Want
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:2 -->
To leave his empty country for London.

### What he wants there
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:4 -->
Harker sees it in chapter 4: a city where, "amongst its teeming
millions," he can feed and make new creatures.

## Relationships
