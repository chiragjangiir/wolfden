# Jonathan Harker

## Overview
A young solicitor sent from Exeter to [[castle-dracula]] to complete the
sale of a London house to [[count-dracula]]. He keeps his journal in
shorthand, which opens the novel. Engaged to [[mina-harker|Mina]].

## Appearance
<!-- wolfden: until=dracula:23 -->
A frank, happy-looking young man with a strong face and dark brown hair.

## Appearance
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:23 -->
After the night the Count attacks Mina, his hair turns white, and Seward
sees "a drawn, haggard old man" with burning eyes.

## Backstory
Newly qualified, he works for Peter Hawkins, a solicitor of Exeter who
treats him almost as a son.

## Want
To finish the Count's business, go home, and marry Mina.

### To destroy the Count
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:22 -->
After the attack on Mina, all he wants is the Count dead, and he says
so.

## Wound
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:8 -->
His weeks as the Count's prisoner end in a brain fever in a hospital in
Buda-Pesth. Afterwards he can't tell whether what he wrote at the castle
was real or madness, and he gives the journal to Mina sealed, asking
never to know.

## Relationships
- [[mina-harker]]: his fiancée, later his wife.
- [[count-dracula]]: his host and jailer.

### The band
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:14 -->
- [[abraham-van-helsing]] gives him back his nerve by believing his
  journal.

## Arc
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:14 -->
From a man who doubts his own mind to one of the hunters. He tracks the
Count's boxes of earth across London.

### Piccadilly
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:23 -->
He slashes at the Count with his [[harkers-kukri]] and nearly has him.

### The end
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:27 -->
At sunset on 6 November he cuts the Count's throat as Morris's knife goes
into the heart.

### Seven years on
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:note -->
He writes the closing Note: he and Mina have a son, [[quincey-harker]].
