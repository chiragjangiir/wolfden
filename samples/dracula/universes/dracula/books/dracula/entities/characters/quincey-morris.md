# Quincey P. Morris

## Overview
A Texan adventurer and friend of [[arthur-holmwood]] and [[john-seward]].
He proposes to [[lucy-westenra|Lucy]] on the same day as Seward and takes
her refusal with grace.

## Voice
American slang and tall talk, which Lucy loves to repeat to Mina.

## Arc
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:12 -->
He gives Lucy his blood, and stays at her side to the end.

### Death
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:27 -->
Stabbed by the Count's gypsy guards in the last fight, he drives his
[[morris-bowie-knife]] into the Count's heart and dies at sunset,
glad that the scar has gone from Mina's forehead: "Now God be thanked
that all has not been in vain!"
