# John Seward

## Overview
A young doctor, "only nine-and-twenty," who runs [[seward-asylum]] at
Purfleet. He keeps his diary on a phonograph, and his record of
[[renfield]] runs through the book.

## Want
[[lucy-westenra|Lucy]], who refuses him on 24 May. Afterwards, work: "the
only cure for this sort of thing."

## Voice
Clinical and self-watchful, though grief keeps breaking through. Prone to
taking chloral to sleep.

## Relationships
- [[lucy-westenra]]: the woman he loves, who refuses him kindly.
- [[arthur-holmwood]] and [[quincey-morris]]: his friends and rivals.

### His old teacher
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:9 -->
- [[abraham-van-helsing]]: his old master from Amsterdam, whom he calls
  in to see Lucy.

## Arc
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:9 -->
A man of science who learns, through Lucy's death and return, to believe
what Van Helsing tells him.
