# Mina Harker

## Overview
Wilhelmina Murray, engaged to [[jonathan-harker]]. Calm, practical, and
quick. Jonathan mentions her on the first page of the novel, and she
writes much of what follows.

## Want
To be useful to Jonathan, and to be a good wife to him.

## Voice
Warm and orderly. She dates everything, copies everything, and makes
lists.

### Madam Mina
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:14 -->
Van Helsing calls her "Madam Mina."

## Relationships
- [[jonathan-harker]]: her fiancé.

### Marriage
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:9 -->
From 24 August, Jonathan's wife.

### Lucy
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:5 -->
- [[lucy-westenra]]: her dearest friend since childhood.

### The band
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:14 -->
- [[abraham-van-helsing]] trusts her mind more than any man's in the
  band.

## Arc
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:9 -->
She marries Jonathan in the hospital at Buda-Pesth on 24 August.

### The archivist
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:17 -->
She types up every journal, letter, and phonograph cylinder in date
order, and so gives the band its first full picture of the Count.

### The Count's victim
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:21 -->
Shut out of the hunt "for her safety," she is left alone at the asylum,
and the Count reaches her there. He makes her drink his blood.

### The Count's compass
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:23 -->
Under hypnosis at sunrise and sunset she can sense where the Count is:
lapping water, creaking timbers, a ship at sea.

### The promise
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:25 -->
She makes the men promise to kill her if she changes past saving, and
asks Jonathan to read the burial service over her while she lives. In
chapter 26 she works out the Count's route home herself.

## The scar
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:22 until=dracula:note pin=true -->
The Host leaves a red scar on her forehead when Van Helsing touches her
with it: "Unclean! Unclean! Even the Almighty shuns my polluted flesh!"
It fades as the Count dies.
