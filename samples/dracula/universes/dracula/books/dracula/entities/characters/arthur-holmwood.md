# Arthur Holmwood

## Overview
The man [[lucy-westenra|Lucy]] accepts, a young aristocrat and old friend
of [[john-seward]] and [[quincey-morris]].

## Relationships
- [[lucy-westenra]]: his fiancée.

## Arc
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:10 -->
He gives Lucy the first transfusion, and believes for a time that it made
them married in the sight of God.

### Lord Godalming
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:13 -->
His father dies within days of Lucy, and he inherits the title. The
narrators call him Lord Godalming, or Godalming, from here on.

### The stake
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:16 -->
He refuses to believe Van Helsing until he sees Lucy Un-Dead, then drives
the stake himself.

### The band's purse
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:18 -->
His money and rank open doors, buy information, and hire the steam
launch for the chase in chapter 26.
