# The little band

## Overview
The six who hunt the Count: Van Helsing, Seward, Jonathan and Mina
Harker, Godalming, and Morris. Jonathan's Note calls them "our little band
of men."

## Goals
To find and sterilize every box of earth the Count brought to England,
then to destroy him.

## Methods
Mina's record of every document, in date order; Godalming's money;
Morris's rifles; Van Helsing's lore; and, after the Count's attack,
Mina's hypnotic link to him.

## Members
Each member's page lists the band in its `factions` field. Mina is shut
out of the band's councils for a time in October, for her own safety,
and let back in once they see the cost.

## History
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:18 -->
They swear themselves to the hunt on 30 September, around the table in
Seward's study.

### The chase east
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:25 -->
From October they follow the Count east: Varna, then Galatz, then three
parties by river, road, and rail to the castle.
