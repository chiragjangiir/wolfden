# The Count in Piccadilly

## Overview
Walking down Piccadilly after Mr. Hawkins's funeral, Jonathan grips
Mina's arm and stares at a tall, thin man with a pointed beard who is
watching a pretty girl in a carriage. "I believe it is the Count, but he
has grown young."

## Consequences
The shock brings back Jonathan's fear of his own madness. Mina decides to
read his sealed journal.
