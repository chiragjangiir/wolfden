# Death of the Count

## Overview
The Szgany race the last box toward the castle with the sun going down.
The four men ride them down, rifles raised. Jonathan and Morris cut
through to the cart, throw the box down, and force the lid. As the
Count's eyes see the setting sun and turn to triumph, Jonathan's
[[harkers-kukri|knife]] shears through his throat and Morris's
[[morris-bowie-knife|bowie knife]] plunges into his heart. The body
crumbles into dust, and Mina sees a look of peace on the face.

## Consequences
Morris, stabbed in the fight, dies at sunset, glad to see that the scar
has gone from Mina's forehead. See [[the-counts-end]] for how the death
squares with the rules Van Helsing laid down.
