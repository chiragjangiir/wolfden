# Lucy on the East Cliff

## Overview
Mina wakes to find Lucy's bed empty and runs through the town in the
moonlight. On the churchyard seat she sees a white figure, and something
long and black bending over it, with a white face and red, gleaming eyes.
When she reaches Lucy, she is alone and asleep, breathing hard.

## Consequences
Next morning there are two tiny red marks on Lucy's throat. Mina thinks
she pricked her with the safety-pin of the shawl.
