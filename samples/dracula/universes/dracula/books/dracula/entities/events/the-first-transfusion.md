# The first transfusion

## Overview
Van Helsing finds Lucy bloodless and dying, and calls for a transfusion
on the spot. Arthur arrives in time to give it.

## Consequences
The first of four. Seward gives his blood on 10 September, Van Helsing
on 13 September, and Morris on 18 September.
