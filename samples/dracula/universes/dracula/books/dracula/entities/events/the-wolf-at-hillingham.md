# The wolf at Hillingham

## Overview
A grey wolf, escaped that afternoon from the Zoological Gardens, breaks
the window of Lucy's room. Her mother, sleeping beside her for comfort,
tears the garlic wreath from Lucy's neck in her terror and dies of a
heart attack. A mist of specks fills the room. Lucy writes it all down
and hides the paper in her breast.

## Causes
The Count needed the garlic gone and the window open. The keeper's
interview in *The Pall Mall Gazette* (dated the next day, but printed
earlier in the chapter) tells how the wolf got out.

## Consequences
Seward arrives early the next morning, can get no answer at the door,
and finds Lucy nearly dead beside her mother's body.
