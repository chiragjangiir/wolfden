# Death of Lucy

## Overview
Arthur is called to say goodbye. As he bends to kiss her, Lucy's voice
turns soft and voluptuous, and Van Helsing hurls him back. A moment
later she is herself again, thanks Van Helsing, and dies.

## Consequences
Seward: "There is peace for her at last. It is the end!"

Van Helsing: "Not so; alas! not so. It is only the beginning!"
