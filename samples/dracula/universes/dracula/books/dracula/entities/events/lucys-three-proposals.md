# Lucy's three proposals

## Overview
In one day Seward and Morris propose to Lucy and are refused, and Arthur
proposes and is accepted. "Why can't they let a girl marry three men, or
as many as want her, and save all this trouble?"

## Consequences
The three men stay friends.

### Blood
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:12 -->
All three will give Lucy their blood.
