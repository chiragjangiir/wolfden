# Lucy is laid to rest

## Overview
The night before, the four men watched the Un-Dead Lucy come back to her
tomb with a child, and slip in through a crack in the door. In daylight
Arthur drives a stake through her heart while Van Helsing reads the
prayer for the dead. Then they cut off her head and fill the mouth with
garlic.

## Consequences
Her face is her own again, and Arthur is at peace. Van Helsing turns the
four of them toward the one who made her.
