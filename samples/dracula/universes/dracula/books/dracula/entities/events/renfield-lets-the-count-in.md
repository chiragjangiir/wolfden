# Renfield lets the Count in

## Overview
Told by Renfield as he dies: the Count came to his window, raised his
hand over thousands of rats with red eyes: "All these lives will I give
you, ay, and many more and greater." Renfield opened the sash: "Come
in, Lord and Master!" That gave him the right to enter the house where
Mina was sleeping.

## Causes
The rule that the Un-Dead may not enter a house unless invited (see
[[rules-of-the-un-dead]]).

## Consequences
The Count comes and goes in the asylum at will. See
[[the-count-visits-mina]].

This happens in-world before the hunters' first raid on Carfax, but the
reader only learns of it in chapter 21: the clearest gap in the novel
between `when` and `from`.
