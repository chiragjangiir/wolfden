# Harker arrives at the castle

## Overview
On St. George's Eve Harker leaves [[bistritz]] by coach. At the Borgo Pass
a calèche with a tall, silent driver takes him on through the night, past
blue flames and a ring of wolves that the driver waves away. Before dawn
he reaches the castle, and an old man in black lets him in.

## Consequences
The driver's strength and his way with wolves are the first hints that he
and the Count are the same man.
