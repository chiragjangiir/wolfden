# Mina and Jonathan marry

## Overview
Mina travels to Buda-Pesth and marries Jonathan at his bedside in the
hospital, before the chaplain of the English mission church. Jonathan
gives her his journal and asks never to know what is in it.

## Consequences
Her journal is headed Mina Harker's from chapter 13 (22 September) on.
