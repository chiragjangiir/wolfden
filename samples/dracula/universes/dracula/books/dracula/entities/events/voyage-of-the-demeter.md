# The voyage of the Demeter

## Overview
The Russian schooner *Demeter* sails from Varna with silver sand and
boxes of earth. Her crew disappear one by one on the way, and in a sudden
storm she runs into Whitby harbour with only her dead captain aboard,
lashed to the wheel with a crucifix in his hands. A great dog leaps
ashore and is gone.

## Causes
The Count needs to reach England with his earth.

## Consequences
The [[earth-boxes]] land at Whitby. Within days Mr. Swales is dead and
Lucy is walking in her sleep.
