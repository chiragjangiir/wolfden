# The sisters destroyed

## Overview
On the night of 4 November the three women circle Van Helsing's ring of
crumbled Host in the snow, calling to Mina as "sister," but cannot cross
it. Next day he goes into the castle alone, finds them sleeping in their
tombs, and does to each what was done to Lucy.

## Consequences
He seals the Count's own tomb with the Host, so the Count has nowhere left
to go but his last box.
