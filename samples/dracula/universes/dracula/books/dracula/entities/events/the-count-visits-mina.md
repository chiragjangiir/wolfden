# The Count visits Mina

## Overview
Warned by the dying Renfield, the men break into the Harkers' room.
Jonathan lies in a stupor on the bed. The Count holds Mina by the back of
the neck and forces her mouth to a cut in his breast. Van Helsing drives
him back with the Host, and he is gone as a mist.

## Causes
[[renfield-lets-the-count-in]]. The men had left Mina alone at night to
keep her safe from the hunt.

## Consequences
The Count has made her drink his blood: "flesh of my flesh; blood of my
blood; kin of my kin." She tells it all herself, and her first words
after are "Unclean, unclean!"
