# Harker escapes the castle

## Overview
With the Count gone and the Szgany carting off the last boxes, Harker is
alone with the three women: "Mina is a woman, and there is nought in
common. They are devils of the Pit!" He sets out to climb down the castle
wall, and his journal stops.

## Causes
The Count has promised him to the women: "To-night is mine. To-morrow
night is yours!"

## Consequences
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:8 -->
He reaches a hospital in Buda-Pesth with a violent brain fever, and is
there for nearly six weeks before anyone at home knows it.
