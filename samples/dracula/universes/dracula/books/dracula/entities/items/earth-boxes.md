# The boxes of earth

## Overview
Fifty great wooden boxes filled with earth from the Count's own ground.
He can rest only in that earth, so these are his lairs in England, and
the band's way to corner him.

## Description
Heavy, square boxes, each filled with mould dug from the castle grounds,
and big enough to hold a man. Harker sees the Count asleep in one,
"gorged with blood."

## History
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:4 -->
In June the Szgany fill them at the castle and carry them away.

### England
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:7 -->
They cross to [[whitby]] as the *Demeter*'s cargo, then go by rail to
London and on to [[carfax]].

### Scattered
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:19 -->
When the band searches Carfax only twenty-nine are left.

### Traced
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:20 -->
Jonathan traces the rest, through carters and their books, to
Bermondsey, Mile End, and [[piccadilly-house]].

### The last box
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:24 -->
The band sterilizes every box it finds. One is missing, and the Count
takes it home by sea in the *Czarina Catherine*.
