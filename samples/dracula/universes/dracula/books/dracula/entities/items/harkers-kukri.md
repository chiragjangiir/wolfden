# Harker's Kukri knife

## Overview
The great curved Gurkha knife Jonathan carries in the hunt.

## History
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:23 -->
At Piccadilly Jonathan makes "a fierce and sudden cut" at the Count. The
blade misses his heart by a hair and slits his coat, and bank-notes and
gold spill out.

### The end
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:27 -->
At sunset on 6 November it shears through the Count's throat as
[[morris-bowie-knife|Morris's bowie knife]] goes into his heart.
