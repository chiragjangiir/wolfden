# Rules of the Un-Dead

What the novel establishes about vampires, mostly from Van Helsing's
briefing to the band in chapter 18. These are the facts to check before
writing any scene with the Count in it.

## Powers
<!-- wolfden: pin=true -->
- Has the strength of many men, and grows younger by feeding.
- Casts no shadow and no reflection: "he make in the mirror no reflect."
- Can become a wolf, a bat, or a mist, and can command rats, wolves, and
  the weather near him.
- Can see in the dark, and can slip through the smallest crack.

## Limits
<!-- wolfden: pin=true -->
- Can't enter a house unless someone of the household first invites him
  in.
- His power fails at dawn. By day he can change his shape only at sunrise,
  noon, or sunset.
- Can cross running water only at slack or flood tide, or be carried
  over it.
- Must rest in earth from his homeland, which is why he ships fifty boxes
  of it to England.
- Garlic, the crucifix, and the Host repel him. A branch of wild rose on
  his coffin keeps him in it.

## Destroying one
<!-- wolfden: pin=true -->
A stake through the heart, then the head cut off and the mouth filled
with garlic. Lucy is destroyed this way.
