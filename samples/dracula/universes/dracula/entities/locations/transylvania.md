# Transylvania

## Overview
The mountain country on the eastern edge of Europe where the Count has his
castle. Harker finds no accurate map of it, and the people of
[[bistritz]] cross themselves at the castle's name.

## Description
Forests, gorges, and the Carpathian passes, with wolves in the hills. In
Harker's words, "every known superstition in the world is gathered into
the horseshoe of the Carpathians."
