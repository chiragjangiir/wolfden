# The house in Piccadilly

## Overview
No. 347, Piccadilly, a house the Count buys under the name "Count de
Ville" and uses as his base in the West End. [[jonathan-harker]] traces
the missing boxes here.

## History
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:22 -->
The band breaks in on 3 October, finds eight of the nine boxes, and
sterilizes them.

### The ambush
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:23 -->
They wait for the Count here. He comes, Harker's [[harkers-kukri]] slashes
open his coat, and he escapes through the window with a handful of
money.
