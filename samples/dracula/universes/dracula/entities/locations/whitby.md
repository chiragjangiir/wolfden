# Whitby

## Overview
A fishing and holiday town on the Yorkshire coast, circled on a map in
the Count's library.

### A summer by the sea
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:6 -->
Mina and Lucy spend the summer here.

### Landfall
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:7 -->
The Count comes ashore here.

## Description
A harbour between two cliffs. On the East Cliff stand the ruined abbey and
St. Mary's Church, with a churchyard full of sailors' graves and a seat
where Mina likes to sit and write.

## History
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:6 -->
Mina arrives on 24 July and joins Lucy, who is staying with her mother.

### The storm
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:7 -->
On the night of 7–8 August a storm drives the Russian schooner *Demeter*
into the harbour with her captain dead at the wheel, and a great dog
leaps ashore. See [[voyage-of-the-demeter]].
