# Carfax

## Overview
The old estate at [[purfleet]] that Harker sells to the Count. Its name,
Harker guesses, is "a corruption of the old *Quatre Face*," since the
house faces the four points of the compass.

## Description
A great, dark, neglected house behind a high stone wall, part of it as
old as a medieval keep, with an old chapel close by and a deep, dark pond
in the grounds. Its neighbour is
[[seward-asylum]].

## History
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:2 -->
Bought by the Count through Harker's employer before Harker reaches the
castle.

### The boxes arrive
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:8 -->
The fifty [[earth-boxes]] from the *Demeter* are carted here in August
and stacked in the chapel.

### The search
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:19 -->
On 1 October the band breaks in and finds only twenty-nine of the fifty
boxes left. They leave them untouched for now, so as not to warn the
Count.

### Sterilized
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:22 -->
On 3 October they open each box and lay a piece of the Host in the
earth, so the Count can no longer rest in it.
