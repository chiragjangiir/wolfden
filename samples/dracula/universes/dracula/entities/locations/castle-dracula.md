# Castle Dracula

## Overview
The Count's half-ruined castle, high on a precipice above the forests of
[[transylvania]]. The novel opens and ends here.

## Description
A vast building with broken battlements, reached by the Borgo Pass. Most
doors are locked. There are no servants, no mirrors, and, in one
forbidden wing, three women. The Count rests by day in a box of earth in
a ruined chapel below.

## History
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:1 -->
Harker arrives before dawn on 5 May and is kept as a guest, then a
prisoner, until he climbs down the wall on 30 June.

### The hunt's end
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:27 -->
[[abraham-van-helsing]] enters the castle on 5 November, destroys
[[the-weird-sisters]] in their tombs, and seals the Count's own tomb with
the Host. The Count dies the next day at sunset, in sight of the walls.

### Seven years on
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:note -->
The Harkers visit with their son and find the castle standing "as before,
reared high above a waste of desolation."
