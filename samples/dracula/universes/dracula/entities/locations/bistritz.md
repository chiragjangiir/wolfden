# Bistritz

## Overview
The last town on Harker's road to the castle. He stays at the Golden Krone
Hotel on the night of 3 May and leaves on 4 May, the eve of St. George's
Day.

## Description
An old market town. The landlady begs Harker not to go on, and when he
insists she hangs her own crucifix around his neck.
