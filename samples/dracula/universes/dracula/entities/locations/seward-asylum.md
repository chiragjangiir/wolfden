# Seward's asylum

## Overview
A private lunatic asylum next to [[carfax]]. Harker mentions it to the
Count as a neighbour that "is not, however, visible from the grounds."

## History
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:5 -->
Run by [[john-seward]], who keeps [[renfield]] here as his most
interesting patient.

### Headquarters
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:17 -->
From the end of September the band lives and plans here, since Carfax is
next door. Mina stays here too, which is how the Count reaches her.
