# London

## Overview
England's capital, which the Count studies in his library before he
comes. Most of the middle of the novel happens in and around it.

### The Count's design
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:4 -->
The city the Count means to make his hunting ground: "teeming millions"
and nobody who knows what he is.

## Description
A city of districts the story keeps returning to.

### Hampstead
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:13 -->
Hampstead to the north, where Lucy lived and is buried.

### The Count's London
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:20 -->
Piccadilly in the West End, where the Count keeps a house; and the docks
and the East End, where his boxes are hidden.
