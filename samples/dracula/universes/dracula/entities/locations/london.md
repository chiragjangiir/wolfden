# London

## Overview
The city the Count means to make his hunting ground: "teeming millions"
and nobody who knows what he is. Most of the middle of the novel happens
in and around it.

## Description
Hampstead to the north, where Lucy lives and is buried; Piccadilly in the
West End, where the Count keeps a house; and the docks and the East End,
where his boxes are hidden.
