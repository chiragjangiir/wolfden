# The Westenra tomb

## Overview
The family vault in a lonely churchyard near Hampstead Heath where Lucy
and her mother are laid. The churchyard is named as Kingstead in
chapter 15.

## History
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:15 -->
Van Helsing and Seward open it at night and find Lucy's coffin empty, then
by day find her in it, more beautiful than in life.

### The staking
<!-- wolfden: from=dracula:16 -->
Arthur frees Lucy here on 29 September. See [[lucy-is-laid-to-rest]].
