The world of Bram Stoker's *Dracula* (1897): a Transylvanian count buys a
house near London, and a solicitor, a schoolmistress, a doctor, a
professor, a lord, and a Texan hunt him back to his castle.

The novel is told in journals, letters, telegrams, newspaper cuttings, and
phonograph diaries, each dated by day and month. How this den handles
those dates, and the year the novel never names, is in
[[dates-and-the-year]].

Places live here at universe scope. The people, events, and threads of
the novel live in the book.
